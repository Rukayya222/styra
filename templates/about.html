Our story

Fashion that knows you.

Built on the belief that real personalisation means knowing your body, your culture, and your moment — not just your click history.

Why Styra exists

Most digital fashion platforms treat personalisation as secondary. They recommend what is popular, what is in stock, or what earns a commission. Styra does none of that. Every outfit in our catalogue is manually reviewed and tagged — body-type suitability, modesty classification, skin-tone colour compatibility, and occasion relevance. When you complete the style quiz, we score every outfit against your exact profile and return the two that match best, with an explanation for each.

Built for Nigerian women

Styra was designed with the full spectrum of how Nigerian women dress in mind. Traditional attire and English fashion. Fully modest and expressively bold. Owambe and office. We do not think these things need separate apps. They belong together.

How recommendations work

We use a rule-based scoring engine — not a black-box algorithm. This means every recommendation can be explained. You will always know why an outfit was chosen for you. Body type is the primary signal. Modesty preference second. Skin-tone colour harmony third.
